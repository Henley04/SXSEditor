Singer Market 歌手市场

The Singer Market is a community-driven marketplace where SXSEditor users can browse, upload, and download .sxssinger voice profiles. It is opened from the main toolbar via the Singer Market button.

歌手市场 是一个由社区驱动的交流平台，SXSEditor 用户可以在其中浏览、上传和下载 .sxssinger 歌手声音配置文件。可通过主工具栏上的 歌手市场 按钮打开。

Features 功能

  • Browse public singers uploaded by the community.
  • 浏览 社区用户上传的公开歌手。
  • Search by name or description.
  • 按名称或描述 搜索。
  • Filter by tags to find the right voice quickly.
  • 按标签 筛选，快速找到合适的声音。
  • Upload your own singers to share with the community (login required).
  • 上传 你的歌手与社区分享（需登录）。
  • Download singers to your machine for use in projects.
  • 下载 歌手到本地以在项目中使用。
